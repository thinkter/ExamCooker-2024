# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ExamCooker serves VIT students who need fast access to exam resources under time pressure, especially near quizzes, CATs, and finals. The primary user is a student trying to find a course, open past papers, notes, syllabus, or curated resources quickly. Secondary users include contributors and moderators who upload, review, and maintain the resource library.

## Product Purpose

ExamCooker collects the academic material students normally hunt for across chats, drives, and scattered links into one reliable place. Success means a student can arrive stressed, search for a course, and reach useful material in seconds without learning a complex system.

## Brand Personality

Fast, student-native, and useful. The voice can be playful about cramming, but the interface should feel competent and direct when students are trying to get work done.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic edtech polish, corporate LMS styling, cluttered university portal layouts, childish study-app visuals, oversized marketing blocks that bury the tool, and scroll gimmicks that make the page feel heavier than the product.

## Design Principles

Lead with the tool. Search and resource discovery are the product's center of gravity.

Keep the crammer's pace. Reduce distance between intent and resource, especially on mobile.

Use student energy without losing trust. The brand can be lively, but the UI should not become noisy or unserious.

Make marketing earn its space. Landing content should reinforce confidence in the resource library without overwhelming the main workflow.

Preserve useful motion. Animated surfaces should add atmosphere and orientation, not make the page feel slow or bulky.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, readable mobile text, and touch targets of at least 44px. Respect reduced motion preferences for continuous animation and avoid relying on color alone to communicate meaning.
